[ 02 / how we operate ]

OPERATORS,
NOT
SPECTATORS.

001

Build it ourselves

Every company in the group is one we started and still run. No licensing someone else's model, no absentee ownership.

002

Practitioners first

The quality of a wellness business is the quality of the person in the room. We hire for skill, train continuously, and pay accordingly.

003

One standard, two countries

A session in Canggu and a session in a Sydney office run to the same protocol, the same hygiene standard and the same follow-up.

004

Honest claims only

We describe what a treatment does and what it doesn't. No miracle language, no invented science — recovery works without the theatre.

[ 03 / for workplaces ]

BRING RECOVERY
INTO THE
OFFICE.

We run recurring on-site wellness programs for Australian workplaces — the same therapists, the same day each cycle, booked through a system your people actually use. We are the wellness partner for LinkedIn's Sydney and Melbourne offices.

How the program works →

“A wellbeing budget only works if people actually book it. So we built the program around attendance, not intention.”

  • scope

    Weekly, fortnightly or monthly on-site days — chair or table based, scaled to your floor.

  • coverage

    Sydney and Melbourne CBD and surrounds, with multi-site programs run to one schedule.

  • reporting

    Utilisation and booking data back to your people team after every cycle.
