How the program works

A wellbeing budget only works if people actually book it. So the program is built around attendance, not intention — the same therapists, the same day each cycle, inside your own building.

We are the wellness partner for LinkedIn's Sydney and Melbourne offices, running a recurring on-site program for their teams in both cities.

Weekly, fortnightly or monthly on-site days — chair or table based, scaled to your floor.

Utilisation and booking data back to your people team after every cycle.
