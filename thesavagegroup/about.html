The Savage Group starts, owns and runs wellness companies in two markets — Australia and Bali. We are operators before we are owners.

[ 01 / the group ]

WHAT WE
ACTUALLY
ARE.

The Savage Group is the parent company behind a set of wellness businesses. It exists so those businesses can share what's worth sharing — standards, training, systems, people — while each one stays specialised enough to be genuinely good at its own thing.

In Bali, that means Distrik. Performance Recovery, a recovery studio built for people who train hard. In Australia, it means on-site wellness programs for workplaces in Sydney and Melbourne — including our program as the wellness partner for LinkedIn's offices in both cities.

We're not a fund and we're not a franchise. Every company under the group is one we started and still run day to day.

[ 02 / what we hold to ]

FIVE THINGS
WE DON'T
NEGOTIATE.

  • 001

    The practitioner is the product. No amount of branding covers a bad session. We hire for skill, keep training, and keep the same people in the room long enough for clients to trust them.

  • 002

    Say what it does. Recovery is well served by honest description. We won't claim a treatment cures what it doesn't, and we'd rather lose a booking than oversell one.

  • 003

    One standard across borders. A session in Canggu and a session on a Sydney office floor run to the same protocol, hygiene standard and follow-up.

  • 004

    Show up on the same day. Consistency beats intensity — in recovery and in how we run a program. Fixed schedules, same faces, no surprises.

  • 005

    Own the operation. If our name is on the door, our people are behind it and the accountability is ours.
