02 / 04 — corporate wellness

Recovery, delivered to your floor.

Recurring on-site wellness programs for Sydney and Melbourne workplaces. We are the wellness partner for LinkedIn's Sydney and Melbourne offices.

the problem01 / 05

Mostwellbeingbudgetsgo unused.

Vouchers expire. Apps go unopened. A one-off wellbeing day in March is forgotten by April. The spend lands on the P&L; the benefit never lands on the team.

On-site works because it removes every step between intention and treatment. Nobody has to book externally, travel, claim back, or use their own time. The therapist is in the building, on a day people already know about.

Our job is to make the program predictable enough to become a habit — the same faces, the same rhythm, month after month.

Enquire about a program
how it works02 / 05
step 001

Scope the floor

We look at headcount, available space and how your people actually work. That sets session length, therapist numbers and whether we run chair-based, table-based or both.

step 002

Set the rhythm

Weekly, fortnightly or monthly — locked into the calendar for the whole cycle so your team can plan around it. Multi-site clients run to one national schedule.

step 003

We run the day

Our therapists arrive with their own equipment, set up, run the bookings and pack down. Your people team does nothing on the day.

step 004

Report back

After each cycle you get utilisation, booking and no-show data — so the program can be defended at budget time with numbers rather than vibes.
