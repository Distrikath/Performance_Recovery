venture 001distrik.

Distrik.Canggu.

Visit distrikath.com

A performance recovery studio in Canggu built for people who train — athletes, surfers, lifters, fighters, and anyone whose body is carrying the cost of a hard week.

The studio runs on a resident team of therapists, so clients choose by approach rather than taking whoever is free. Recovery modalities, IV therapy and blood diagnostics sit under one roof.

therapy
Deep tissue and sports massage, trigger point work, cupping, assisted stretching, joint mobility.
recovery
Ice bath, compression, and structured post-training recovery protocols.
clinical
IV therapy and blood work, for recovery guided by data rather than guesswork.
