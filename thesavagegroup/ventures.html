[ venture 01 ]

DISTRIK.
PERFORMANCE
RECOVERY

Canggu, Bali · Operating

Visit distrikath.com →

Distrik is a performance recovery studio in Canggu built for people who train — athletes, surfers, lifters, fighters, and anyone whose body is carrying the cost of a hard week.

The studio runs on a resident team of therapists, each with their own approach, so clients choose the practitioner who matches what their body needs rather than taking whoever is free. Recovery modalities, IV therapy and blood diagnostics sit under one roof.

  • therapy

    Deep tissue and sports massage, trigger point work, cupping, assisted stretching, joint mobility and alignment-focused bodywork.

  • recovery

    Ice bath, compression and structured post-training recovery protocols.

  • clinical

    IV therapy and blood work, for clients who want their recovery guided by data rather than guesswork.

  • location

    Jl. Raya Babakan Canggu No.4, Kabupaten Badung, Bali — walk-in and online booking.

[ venture 02 ]

CORPORATE
WELLNESS

Sydney & Melbourne · Operating

The program →

The group's Australian arm brings recovery into the workplace. We are the wellness partner for LinkedIn's Sydney and Melbourne offices, delivering a recurring on-site program for their teams in both cities.

The model is deliberately simple: the same therapists, on a fixed schedule, with a booking system staff can use in ten seconds. Consistency is what turns a wellbeing budget into an actual habit.

  • format

    On-site massage and recovery days — chair or table based, run inside your existing space.

  • cadence

    Weekly, fortnightly or monthly cycles, scaled to headcount and floor size.

  • cities

    Sydney and Melbourne, with multi-site clients run to a single national schedule.

  • reporting

    Utilisation, booking and no-show data returned to your people team each cycle.
